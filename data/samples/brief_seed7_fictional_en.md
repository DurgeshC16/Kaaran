# Evidence Brief: Shivtara

> **SYNTHETIC demo data - not real figures and not a real finding.**

**Question:** Why did Shivtara's maths results fall?

## Trend

**[Strong evidence]** Over 2021-22 to 2025-26, the average maths score in Shivtara fell from 56.0 to 52.7. That is a drop of 3.3 points. Its district rank moved from 4 to 9.
  - *Note: The decline is concentrated in a few schools (11 schools).*

## Election-duty check

**[Weak cause]** In 2024-25, the score in Shivtara went down by 2.0 points. But among the other 6 talukas with the same heavy election duty, 5 did not drop. So election duty looks only weakly linked to the fall.
  - *Note: This is an association; it does not prove a cause.*

## Probable main cause

**[Strong evidence]** Since 2023-24, the 11 schools without a maths teacher fell by 28.0 points on average. Other schools fell by 1.2. These schools account for 66.6% of the decline, so vacant maths posts are the probable main cause.

## Twin taluka

**[Learn from]** Nilwadi is similar to Shivtara in size, enrolment and poverty (134 against 140 schools). Yet its score rose by 6.5 points, and its rank moved from 8 to 3. Kamalpeth is also similar, but its score did not rise (a change of 0.3 points).

## Data warning

**[Red flag]** 23 schools in Shivtara reported full attendance for 4 months in a row (August to November 2024). These records look unreliable and need checking. That is 16.4% of the taluka's schools, against 2.6% elsewhere in the district.
  - *Note: Suspicious records were left out of the analysis.*

**[Checked, fine]** 19 schools in Shivtara have low attendance, but it looks like natural ups and downs. They were checked and are not flagged.
