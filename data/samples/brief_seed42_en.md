# Evidence Brief: Pandharpur

> **SYNTHETIC demo data - not real figures and not a real finding.**

**Question:** Why did Pandharpur's maths results fall?

## Trend

**[Strong evidence]** Over 2021-22 to 2025-26, the average maths score in Pandharpur fell from 56.0 to 52.7, a drop of 3.3 points. Its district rank moved from 4 to 9.
  - *Note: The decline is concentrated in a few schools (11 schools).*

## Election-duty check

**[Weak cause]** In 2024-25, the score in Pandharpur went down by 2.0 points. But of the 6 other talukas with the same heavy election duty, 5 did not drop. Election duty looks only weakly linked to the fall.
  - *Note: This is an association; it does not prove a cause.*

## Probable main cause

**[Strong evidence]** Since 2023-24, the 11 schools without a maths teacher fell by 28.0 points on average, against 1.2 for the other schools. These schools account for 66.5% of the taluka's decline. The vacant maths posts are the probable main cause.

## Twin taluka

**[Learn from]** Malshiras is similar to Pandharpur in size, enrolment and poverty, with 134 against 140 schools. Yet its score rose by 6.5 points and its rank moved from 8 to 3. Sangola is also similar, but its score did not rise, a change of 0.3 points.

## Data warning

**[Red flag]** 23 schools in Pandharpur reported full attendance for 4 months in a row (August to November 2024). These records look unreliable and need checking. That is 16.4% of the taluka's schools, against 2.6% elsewhere in the district.
  - *Note: Suspicious records were left out of the analysis.*

**[Checked, fine]** 19 schools in Pandharpur have low attendance, but it looks like natural variation. They were checked and are not flagged.

## Suggested action

- **[Action]** Redeploy maths teachers to the 11 schools that have none. — *District Education Officer (DEO), this month*

- **[Action]** Organise an exposure visit to Malshiras to learn what it did differently. — *Block Education Officer (BEO), this month*

- **[Action]** Check the attendance registers of the 23 schools with full attendance in person. — *Kendra Pramukh (cluster head), this week*

## Other checks

**[Not enough data]** Attendance or score data is incomplete for 46 schools in Mangalvedha, which is 46.0% of its schools. The data should be completed before drawing conclusions.

**[No link found]** No link was found between infrastructure and the change in scores. Karmala, which has the weakest infrastructure, stayed almost level, with a change of 0.0 points.
  - *Note: Infrastructure data is a single-year snapshot.*

**[Not enough data]** There is not enough evidence to judge whether attendance is linked to the score change.
